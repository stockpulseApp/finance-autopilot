## 1) X Thread (10 tweets max)

1/ Self-custody crypto = no exchange risk, but 100% personal responsibility. One mistake = permanent loss. No customer service. No recovery. Just you and your seed phrase.

2/ The real story: exchange hacks have erased billions (Mt. Gox, FTX). But self-custody losses—lost seed phrases, flooded backups, forgotten passwords—are just as devastating and never make headlines.

3/ Setup > everything else. Your initial security decisions matter infinitely more than anything you do after. One bad choice early = seven figures gone forever. Here's the framework that works:

4/ Layer 1: Choose vetted hardware wallets (Ledger, Trezor, ColdCard) or open-source software wallets (Sparrow, MetaMask). Closed-source unknowns = phishing traps designed to steal your seed phrase.

5/ Layer 2: Write your seed phrase on metal stamped plates (Cryptotag, Billfodl), NOT paper/screenshots/cloud. Before trusting it with real money, delete & reinstall wallet to verify backup works.

6/ Layer 3: Create 2+ identical copies in different geographic locations (home safe + bank deposit box + trusted family member). Fire destroys one location; you're covered from another.

7/ Layer 4: Use new addresses for every transaction (most modern wallets do this automatically). Avoid address reuse—it's privacy hygiene + defense against future quantum threats.

8/ Layer 5: Test with $100 first. Two-way transaction. Verify you can receive, send, and that your backup works. Only then move serious money. Costs $0, saves catastrophes.

9/ What self-custody DOESN'T protect: phishing. Social engineering. A fake "wallet verification" website that steals your seed phrase. These are the #1 loss vector, not bad backups.

10/ If you die, your crypto dies unless someone knows where your seed phrase is. Build an inheritance plan. Sealed letter with lawyer. Safe deposit box. Make recovery possible for heirs.
