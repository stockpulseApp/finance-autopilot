8/ Real talk: 82% of wealth comes from investment growth, not salary. Your paycheck funds your life. Your *account sequence* builds your net worth. Get the order right.

9/ The framework: rank by (1) tax benefit and (2) flexibility. HSA wins on both. After that, it's about your income, family situation, and access to these accounts.
