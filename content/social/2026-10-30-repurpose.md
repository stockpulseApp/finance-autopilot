## 1) X Thread (10 tweets max)

```
THREAD: Self-Custody Crypto Safety—Avoid the $2.56M Mistakes

1/ Self-custody = ultimate control. But one mistake = total loss.
   No fraud department. No undo button. No second chances.
   
   $2.56M lost to phishing. Coldcard wallets hacked. Even "doing everything right" isn't enough.
   
   Here's what actually works.

2/ The promise: You hold the keys, you hold the money. No bank fees. No frozen accounts.
   
   The reality: You're responsible for everything. Lose your seed phrase? Gone. Fall for phishing? Irreversible.
   
   For 5-6 figure holdings, it's still worth it. For $500? Use an exchange.

3/ MISTAKE #1: Storing seed phrases digitally
   
   Photos. Cloud. Password managers. All online = all compromised.
   
   FIX: Metal plate or paper. Two copies. Two locations (home + trusted relative).
   Never type the words into any internet-connected device. Ever.

4/ MISTAKE #2: Never testing your backup
   
   You write down 24 words. Six months later: "Wait, is it 'stake' or 'steak'?"
   
   Too late now.
   
   FIX: Immediately wipe your wallet and restore from seed phrase alone. If it works, you're good. If not—fix it while you still have the device.

5/ MISTAKE #3: Blindly signing transactions
   
   Malicious server feeds your wallet fake history. You click "approve." You just signed your entire balance to a hacker.
   
   FIX: Verify COMPLETE address + EXACT amount on your hardware wallet's screen. Not your computer. THE DEVICE.

6/ MISTAKE #4: Downloading wallet software from the wrong place
   
   One paid Google ad. One typo in the URL. Clipboard-hijacking malware installed.
   
   FIX: Only download from official websites (typed directly, not clicked) or verified app stores. Check URLs carefully.

7/ MISTAKE #5: Clicking phishing links
   
   "Your wallet compromised—verify now!" 70 users clicked. $2.56M gone in minutes.
   
   FIX: Treat every alert as a scam. Never click links. Open your wallet directly. Check the blockchain, not messages.
   Disable push notifications entirely.

8/ MISTAKE #6: No inheritance plan
   
   You die with $100K in crypto. Your family can't access it. Keys are lost forever.
   
   FIX: Give a trusted person your seed phrase location (not the phrase itself)—in a safe-deposit box letter opened only after your death.

9/ The tools that work:
   • Hardware wallets (Ledger, Trezor): $60-150, offline keys
   • Software wallets (MetaMask): Hot money only, testing trades
   • Multisig (Casa, Unchained): Gold standard for $100K+
   
   Choose based on amount + risk tolerance.

10/ Bottom line: Self-custody is unforgiving. Setup matters more than anything after.
   
   Use reputable tools. Test backups. Verify every transaction. Never trust links.
   
   Do it right = sleep better. Do it wrong = lose everything.
   
   Choose wisely.
```
