## 1) X Thread (10 tweets max)

```
1/ Self-custody = financial sovereignty, until one typo costs you everything. No "forgot password?" on blockchain. Here's when to move crypto off exchanges and how to do it without expensive mistakes.

2/ The trade-off: exchanges = counterparty risk (hacks, freezes, collapse). Self-custody = operational risk (you lose the key, you lose the crypto). Which matters more to you?

3/ Threshold check: self-custody makes sense when:
   • Holdings exceed $1-2K
   • You plan to hold months/years
   • You understand seed phrases ≠ private keys
   • You're ready to be your own bank

4/ Below $1K? Exchange custody with 2FA might be smarter. Withdrawal fees alone can burn 11.5% of tiny balances. DCA until the math makes sense.

5/ Hardware wallet (Ledger, Trezor) > software wallet for large holdings. Keys stay offline. Computer shows transactions. Device signs them. Private keys never touch internet.

6/ The seed phrase is everything. 12-24 words = ability to regenerate every key your wallet creates. Write on paper or metal. Never screenshot. Store 2+ copies in separate locations.

7/ Test recovery before moving real money. Send $10, wipe device, restore from seed, confirm you can access funds. This 15-minute stress test saves thousands.

8/ Withdrawal fees matter. Bitcoin network fee: $4.50. Ethereum: $3.20. Withdraw during low-congestion periods (weekends) to minimize costs. Check mempool.space first.

9/ Address verification is obsessive-compulsive for a reason: blockchains are irreversible. Wrong address = gone forever. Check first 6 and last 6 characters. Send test transaction first.

10/ Moving crypto to self-custody isn't complicated—it's just unforgiving. One mistake is permanent. Read the guides. Test everything. Move slow. Then sleep better knowing you actually own it.
```
