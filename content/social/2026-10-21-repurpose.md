## 1) X Thread (10 Tweets Max)

1/ Your crypto is only as safe as your backup plan. 🧵 Every year, thousands lose life savings to ONE mistake—not a market crash, but operational error. Here's how self-custody actually works (and how to avoid becoming a cautionary tale).

2/ The crypto paradox: exchanges are risky (FTX, Mt. Gox, Bybit breach = $1.4B lost). Self-custody removes that risk—but it's unforgiving. You become the vault. No "forgot password" button. No customer service. Just you and your seed phrase.

3/ MISTAKE #1: Storing seed phrases digitally (cloud, photos, notes). Your 24 words = total access. One: write on paper immediately. Two: create 2+ exact copies. Three: store geographically separate (home safe + family member's house or bank box).

4/ MISTAKE #2: Never testing recovery before sending real funds. Best practice: send a small test amount, delete the wallet, reinstall it, enter your backup seed phrase. If funds appear, your backup works. Do this BEFORE moving the whole stack.

5/ MISTAKE #3: Sending to wrong address or wrong blockchain. Bitcoin to Ethereum address? Gone forever. No refunds in crypto. Always verify first 6 AND last 6 characters. For large transfers, send a test amount first ($20 worth). Confirm arrival. Then send the rest.

6/ MISTAKE #4: Weak passwords + phishing risks. Use 20+ character passphrases in a password manager. Enable 2FA everywhere. Never enter your seed phrase into websites or browser extensions claiming to "validate" your wallet. Ever.

7/ MISTAKE #5: No disaster plan for heirs/family. If something happens to you, can your family access your crypto? Store a copy of your seed phrase in a will or sealed envelope with trusted instructions. (This one saves fortunes.)

8/ The weekend drill that prevents 95% of losses: 1) Write seed phrase on paper (2 copies). 2) Store copies in separate locations. 3) Send test transaction. 4) Test recovery (delete/reinstall wallet). 5) Test again 6 months later.

9/ Self-custody isn't hard—it's *unforgiving*. But if you follow these 5 steps, the odds of a costly mistake drop to near-zero. Your coins stay *actually* yours. No platform can freeze them. No hack can touch them (if they're offline).

10/ The real question: is self-custody worth the friction? If you're holding for 10+ years, if you have five figures or more, if you don't trust third parties—the answer is yes. Spend 3 hours now. Sleep soundly for a decade. That's the trade.
