## 1. X Thread (10 tweets max)

```
1/ 95% of crypto losses come from USER ERROR—not hacks. 
Screenshots of seed phrases. Cloud storage backups. Wrong addresses.
Here's how to self-custody safely without becoming a statistic.

2/ The golden rule: "Not your keys, not your coins."
But keeping your keys means YOU become the vault.
No customer service. No password resets. No second chances.
Get the setup right the first time.

3/ ⚠️ The failure modes that actually cost people money:
• Single seed phrase in one location (one fire = total loss)
• Digital copies of recovery words (hackers love iCloud screenshots)
• Sending crypto to wrong addresses (irreversible)
• Fake wallet software (phishing wins)

4/ Real example: $70M drained from Coldcard wallets in 41 minutes.
Why? Users photographed seed phrases + malware intercepted clipboard data.
Hardware wallets are safer—but only if you use them correctly.

5/ Strategy: Two-wallet approach
📱 HOT WALLET (software): $200-$2K daily spending
🔒 COLD WALLET (hardware): Everything else in a safe
Convenience + Security = balance.

6/ Seed phrase backup rules (non-negotiable):
✗ No screenshots, cloud storage, or messaging apps
✓ Write on paper with pen
✓ Stamp into metal for fireproofing
✓ Store geographically separated copies
✓ Never photograph it

7/ Test before you commit.
Send $50 to your new wallet. Confirm arrival.
Only then move your full holdings.
Small transaction fees save you from expensive mistakes.

8/ Multisig is worth it for serious money.
Requires 2-of-3 or 3-of-5 signatures to move funds.
One compromised key ≠ total loss.
Complexity tradeoff: Yes. Peace of mind: Priceless.

9/ Never download wallets via search results.
Phishing sites rank high.
Type URLs directly. Use official app stores.
Verify publisher names. Bookmark legitimate sites.

10/ Self-custody isn't hard. It's unforgiving.
Setup right = you own your wealth.
Setup wrong = you own your loss.
Take 90 minutes to get this right. Don't gamble $23K on convenience.
```
