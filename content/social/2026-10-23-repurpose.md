3/ THE TRAP: You transfer $8K to a new card, feel relief, then rack up $2.4K in NEW debt on old cards within 6 months. Now you're managing multiple balances and owe more than you started with. The psychological relief enables the same habits.

5/ THE REAL MATH: Use this formula to know exactly what to transfer: (Monthly payment × Promo months) ÷ 1.03 = Maximum amount. Example: Can afford $400/month for 15 months? Transfer max $5,825. Anything more = interest charges guaranteed.

6/ SMART MOVE: Complete all transfers within 48 hours of approval. Most cards have 4-month windows to qualify for 0%—after that, fees jump or promos don't apply. Don't waste promotional time waiting.

7/ SMART MOVE: Set up automatic minimum payments 3 days before the due date. One late payment kills your entire promotional rate. Autopay removes the risk of human error.

8/ SMART MOVE: Lock away old cards during your payoff period. Freeze them literally or close them. Every new charge = extending your debt timeline and negating your 0% advantage.

9/ THE HONEST TRUTH: A 0% transfer card saved people an average of $1,868 in year one with no fees. But that only works if you: (1) Pay off before promo ends, (2) Don't spend on old cards, (3) Never miss a payment, (4) Know your actual payoff number.

**Body:**

You transfer $8,200 to a new card with 0% APR for 21 months.

On paper, you're saving money. No interest charges. Just principal paydown.

In reality, one of four things usually happens:

❌ **You miss a payment.** Promo rate drops. APR jumps to 24%. Sometimes interest is calculated *retroactively* on your entire original balance.

❌ **You keep spending on old cards.** Relief feels good. New charges follow. You're managing multiple debts instead of consolidating.

❌ **You can't pay it off in time.** You transfer $8,240 (with 3% fee). You need to pay $550/month to clear it. You can only manage $300/month. Revert rates hit—often 20%+.

❌ **You get approved for too small a credit limit.** You wanted to transfer $10,000. You got $6,000. Now you're still paying interest on $4,000 elsewhere.

But here's what works:

✅ Use this formula FIRST: (Monthly payment × Promo months) ÷ 1.03 = Max transfer amount

✅ Freeze or close old cards immediately
