## 1) X Thread (10 tweets max)

```
1/ Most freelancers leave 40-60% of their potential income on the table. Not because they lack talent — because they don't understand their true cost of business. Here's the framework that changed everything.

2/ Your hourly rate isn't your salary. It needs to cover:
- Self-employment tax (15.3%)
- Health insurance
- Retirement
- Business expenses
- Unbillable time (30%+ of your hours)

A freelancer targeting $60K take-home actually needs $87K+ in revenue.

3/ Real math: Want to net $60K/year?
- Add taxes, insurance, retirement: +$23K
- Add business expenses: +$4.5K
- Add unbillable time (30%): massive hit to billable hours
- Result: You need $62/hour MINIMUM just to break even

4/ The fatal flaw of hourly billing: it punishes efficiency. Get faster at your craft → earn less per hour. Build better systems → lower effective rate.

Solution? Stop selling time. Start selling outcomes.

5/ Value-based pricing example:
Two developers build the same site:
- Dev A: $75/hr × 40 hrs = $3,000
- Dev B: $6,500 flat fee × 30 hrs = $217/hr effective

Same result. Dev B earns 3x more because she priced value, not time.

6/ The 5-layer pricing formula:
Layer 1: Cost floor (your break-even)
Layer 2: Market rate (what others charge)
Layer 3: Specialization premium (+20-50% for niches)
Layer 4: Outcome value (what result do you create?)
Layer 5: Scarcity (how booked are you?)

7/ Real example: A copywriter stopped charging $45/hr. She calculated her outcome value (8-12% conversion lift = $24K ARR for clients). New pricing: $3,500-$5,000 per email sequence.

Same work. Income tripled. Hours cut in half.

8/ Don't drop your rate when budgets tighten. One Instagram post nailed it: "Dropping your rate feels kind, but it permanently resets what clients think your work is worth."

Rate cuts are the fastest way to become permanently undervalued.

9/ When to use each model:
- Hourly: Discovery phases, retainers, unclear scope
- Fixed: Defined deliverables, scope boundaries
- Value-based: Measurable business outcomes
- Retainer: Ongoing predictable work

Mix and match by project.

10/ Action step: Calculate your true cost floor TODAY. Plug in:
- Desired income
- Taxes + insurance + retirement
- Business expenses
- Billable hours available (hint: it's 1,400-1,600/year, not 2,080)

Then stop undercharging.
```
