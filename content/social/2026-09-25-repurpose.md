# House Hacking Repurposed Content

## X Thread (10 Tweets)

1/ 🏠 Your mortgage is eating your paycheck. But what if your tenants paid it instead? House hacking flips the script: buy a property, live in one unit, rent the others. Your tenant offsets your housing costs while you build equity. Here's how first-time buyers are doing it.

2/ 📊 The math is compelling. Sarah buys a Cleveland duplex for $310k instead of a $250k single-family home. Her $1,960 mortgage? Cut to $1,240/month after rental income. That's $9,120/year freed up—or $45,600 over five years.

3/ 🏘️ Four house hacking formats work:
• 2-4 unit multifamily (duplex/triplex/fourplex)
• Accessory dwelling units (ADU/garage apartment)
• Room rentals in single-family homes
• Short-term rentals (Airbnb side units)

Pick based on capital, privacy tolerance & local regulations.

4/ 💰 Owner-occupied mortgages are your secret weapon. FHA loans: 3.5% down on properties up to 4 units. Conventional: 3-5% down. VA: 0% down for veterans. And here's the kicker—lenders count 75% of rental income toward your approval. That's how you qualify for bigger properties.

5/ 📍 Property selection makes or breaks the strategy. Hunt for strong rental demand (universities, hospitals, military bases). Target rent-to-price ratios of 0.7%+ and prioritize properties in good condition. A $15k roof replacement kills years of savings.

6/ 🔍 The rent-to-price ratio is your screening tool. Divide monthly rent by purchase price. A $300k duplex renting each unit for $1,200 = 0.8% ratio ($2,400 ÷ $300k). Below 0.7%? You're subsidizing tenants, not breaking even.

7/ 🛠️ Separation = success. Best house hacks have separate entrances, individual utilities, and sound insulation between units. Shared kitchens? That's friction living in your face 24/7. Shared laundry? Fine.

8/ ⚖️ You're now a landlord. Tenant screening isn't optional—it's survival. Run credit checks, verify employment, contact previous landlords. One bad tenant costs more than the extra rent you'd capture by lowering standards.

9/ 📋 Legal compliance matters. Fair housing laws, state landlord-tenant codes, local ordinances—they all apply. Spend $500 on a lawyer to review leases and local rules. Avoid $15k discrimination lawsuits. Budget 8-10% of rent for maintenance and vacancy.

10/ 🚀 House hacking isn't passive income—it's your first real estate move. Live there, manage tenants, build equity, repeat. Start in the Midwest where returns shine, know your local laws, and pick properties with rental separation. Your housing costs? Problem solved.
