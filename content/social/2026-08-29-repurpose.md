## 1) X Thread (10 Tweets)

**Tweet 1**
House hacking: buying a home, living in part of it, and renting the rest to cover your mortgage. Sarah Chen dropped her housing cost from $2,340/month to $1,000. Here's how first-time buyers are using this strategy to build wealth in 2026.

**Tweet 2**
The math: $625k Oakland home = $4,200 mortgage. Rent two bedrooms at $1,600 each = $3,200 income. Net cost: $1,000/month. She's saving $1,340 vs. her old apartment rent + building equity. That's $22,800/year in housing cost savings.

**Tweet 3**
Three house hacking models:
• Room rental (single-family, rent bedrooms)
• Duplex/triplex (live in one unit, rent others)
• ADU (rent a separate backyard or basement apartment)

Each has different privacy/profit trade-offs.

**Tweet 4**
Why house hacking works: owner-occupant financing. FHA loans need just 3.5% down vs. 15-25% for investment properties. Interest rates run 0.5-0.75% lower. That $59,000 in saved down payment? Now your capital for the next property.

**Tweet 5**
Real example: Marcus, 28, Atlanta software engineer earning $95k. Same $380k house. Without house hacking: $2,720/month cost. With two roommates: $820/month. Five-year savings: $114,000. Enough for his next down payment.

**Tweet 6**
The tax angle: Marcus can't deduct the full mortgage. But he can deduct expenses proportional to the rented space—mortgage interest, property taxes, repairs. Taxable rental income drops from $22,800 to ~$4,800. Saves him $1,152 in federal tax annually.

**Tweet 7**
The costs nobody mentions:
• Time (5-10 hours/month as landlord minimum)
• Vacancy (expect 5-7% annual vacancy = lost rent)
• Turnover (rooms turn over annually; duplexes last 12-24 months)
• Maintenance (you can't defer repairs when you live there)

**Tweet 8**
Vacancy math: Rent two $1,600 rooms. Expect 3-4 weeks lost annually per unit. That's $1,200-$1,600 in missing income. Budget for it. Turnover costs: $500-$1,500 per tenant in cleaning, repairs, ads.

**Tweet 9**
FHA & VA loans unlock house hacking for first-time buyers and vets. VA loans: zero down, no mortgage insurance. FHA: 3.5% down, credit score 580+. Own-occupancy requirement: 1 year minimum. Then you move out, rent it all, and repeat.

**Tweet 10**
The strategic move: live in property 1 while renting out most units. After 1 year, move to property 2 (house hacking again). Property 1 becomes full rental covering itself while you build equity in property 2. This is how the wealthy scale fast.
