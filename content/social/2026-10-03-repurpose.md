## 1) X Thread (10 tweets)

```
1/ "Not your keys, not your coins"—but self-custody introduced a new risk: you.

Lost seed phrases. Phishing attacks. Hardware failures. The autonomy that protects you from exchange bankruptcy can trap your life savings behind a forgotten password.

Here's how to do it right. 🧵

2/ First, understand what you're choosing:
- Exchange custody = you trust Coinbase, Kraken, etc. Risk: bankruptcy, hacks, account freezes
- Self-custody = you hold the private keys. Risk: human error, theft, loss

FTX lost $8B in customer funds. Bybit lost $1.4B. This matters.

3/ The custody spectrum:

📱 Mobile wallets (Trust, MetaMask): Daily spending. <$2K. Vulnerable to phone theft & malware.

🔐 Hardware wallets (Ledger, Trezor): $10K+. Keys never touch the internet. You press buttons to confirm txns.

🏠 Multisig vaults: $100K+. Distributed risk.

4/ Your seed phrase is everything.

24 English words = ability to regenerate every private key you own.

Lose it? Your coins are frozen forever.
Share it? A scammer drains your wallet in seconds.

Store it on paper in 2+ geographically separated locations. Never digitally.

5/ The 7 deadly sins of self-custody:

❌ Single backup location (fires happen)
❌ Digital storage (cloud = larger attack surface)
❌ Skipping recovery drills
❌ Trusting phishing emails
❌ Using sketchy wallet software
❌ Forgetting the passphrase
❌ No inheritance plan

6/ Test your backup BEFORE moving serious money.

Wipe your hardware wallet. Restore it with your written seed phrase. If word #11 is illegible, discover it when you've risked $50, not $50K.

This one step prevents most catastrophic losses.

7/ Sarah's setup (Austin, 34, $12K in BTC/ETH):
- Ledger Nano X: $149
- Fireproof safe: $65
- Metal seed backup: $40
- Safe-deposit box: $60/year

Total: ~$300 to protect $38K from exchange bankruptcy.

The math is obvious.

8/ Good news for institutions: the SEC just proposed new custody rules allowing registered funds to self-custody crypto directly.

This legitimizes what retail holders have done for 15 years and signals regulatory acceptance is coming.

9/ Self-custody isn't for everyone:

✅ Use it for: Holdings >$10K, multi-year positions, wealth preservation
❌ Skip it for: Trading capital <$5K, active DeFi, daily spending

Treat the exchange like checking. Treat hardware wallets like savings.

10/ The bottom line: self-custody removes exchange risk but shifts responsibility onto you.

The setup is boring.
The recovery drill is tedious.
But the peace of mind—knowing no bank, no exchange, no hacker can touch your coins—is worth the effort.

Not your keys = not your coins.
```
