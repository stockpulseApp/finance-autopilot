## 1) X Thread (10 tweets)

**Tweet 1:**
Your budget dies around the 12th because unassigned money gets spent accidentally. Zero-based budgeting fixes that: Income − Every Expense − Savings = $0. No leftovers. Every dollar has a job before the month starts.

**Tweet 2:**
The core insight: Money you don't explicitly assign disappears. So why not direct it intentionally? That's zero-based budgeting. It's not about restriction—it's about *agency*.

**Tweet 3:**
Step 1: Calculate true take-home (not gross salary). If you earn $58k gross but take home $1,615 biweekly after taxes & 401(k), that's $3,499/month to budget with. Start with reality, not wishful thinking.

**Tweet 4:**
Step 2: Lock down fixed expenses first (rent, car payment, insurance, subscriptions). This is your floor. You can't negotiate it away, but you can question it for *next* month.

**Tweet 5:**
Step 3: The hard part—assign specific numbers to every variable expense. Not "$250 for food." Try "$480 groceries, $160 gas, $200 restaurants." Specificity is what makes this work.

**Tweet 6:**
Also assign savings, debt payoff, and goals the same way. Your emergency fund isn't a leftover—it's a job assignment getting the same priority as rent. That's why it actually happens.

**Tweet 7:**
Step 4: Track spending as it happens (app, sheet, or paper). Check weekly. A zero-based budget is a hypothesis reality tests daily. Untracked spending = your budget becomes fiction by week 2.

**Tweet 8:**
Step 5: When (not if—*when*) reality intrudes, reallocate consciously. Work shoes broke? Move $35 from entertainment to clothing. Stay at zero. Never spend unassigned money.

**Tweet 9:**
Honest warning: This takes 3-5 hours month one, then 30-60 min/week. If you hate admin work, automate transfers and use an app that imports transactions. Don't let friction kill it.

**Tweet 10:**
Strict zero-based budgeting can backfire if you treat assigned budgets as "spend targets" instead of ceilings. The goal is intentional spending, not maximized spending. Control the money; don't let it control you.
