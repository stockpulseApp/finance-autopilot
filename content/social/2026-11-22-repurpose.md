# Repurposed Content

## 1. X Thread (10 tweets max)

```
1/ Most freelancers underprice by 50%+. Sarah quit her $65K job, calculated she needed $34/hr, then charged $30 to "stay competitive." Six months later? Exhausted and broke.

The difference between scraping by and building real wealth comes down to one number: your pricing strategy.

2/ Here's what most freelancers get wrong:

Hourly billing = you get paid LESS as you get better.
Your skill growth reduces revenue.
Clients focus on hours instead of results.
You become a cost center, not a profit driver.

3/ Do this math instead:
• Target take-home: $65,000
• Add 30% for taxes + benefits: $19,500
• Add business costs: $8,000
• Total needed: $92,500

That's your revenue target. Now divide by ACTUAL billable hours.

4/ Not 2,080 hours/year. That's fantasy.
Realistic freelancer math:
- Vacation/sick: -120 hrs
- Admin, marketing, proposals: -40% (-784 hrs)
- Actual billable time: ~1,176 hrs/year

$92,500 ÷ 1,176 = $78.65/hour MINIMUM.

That's 2.6x the hourly rate you started with.

5/ But hourly is still leaving money on the table.

Value-based pricing: charge based on outcome, not input.
If your work generates $50K in new revenue, a $15K fee looks like a steal—even if it took 30 hours.

6/ The shift in conversation:
❌ "How many hours will this take?"
✅ "What does success look like in revenue/savings?"
✅ "What's this problem worth to your business?"
✅ "What's your budget?"

Client economics = your pricing power.

7/ Project pricing is the middle ground:
- Clear deliverables (not "a website"—be specific)
- Realistic estimates + 20% buffer
- Build revision limits
- Milestone payments: 50/25/25
- Rush premiums for fast turnarounds

8/ Quick wins to raise rates:

🔸 If you're brand new: charge hourly to build confidence, then raise $10-20 after each project
🔸 Unique expertise? Premium applies
🔸 Tight deadline? Rush fee (25-50% extra)
🔸 Measurable ROI? Value-based pricing unlocks margins

9/ The psychology: clients pay more when they see value, not cost.
"This costs $100" triggers friction.
"This generates $500K in new sales, so $15K is 3% of upside" triggers investment thinking.

Reframe everything around their outcome.

10/ Start calculating today.
What's your true minimum rate?
What value do you actually create?
What's your next rate increase?

Pricing isn't about greed. It's about sustainability—and respecting your own worth.
```
