## X Thread (10 tweets)

1/ Markets crashing? Your emotions say run. Your portfolio says stay. There's a strategy for exactly this moment—and it's simpler than you think. Thread on dollar-cost averaging 🧵

2/ Dollar-cost averaging = investing a fixed amount at regular intervals, regardless of market conditions. $500/month, every month. Up market, down market, sideways market. Doesn't matter.

3/ Here's what most people get wrong: DCA doesn't mathematically beat lump-sum investing. Markets go up ~70% of the time, so investing all your money immediately usually wins. On paper.

4/ But here's what actually matters: Most people don't have lump sums. They have paychecks. And when markets drop 20%, they panic-sell. DCA removes that emotional decision entirely.

5/ Real example: Investor Maya put $750/month into VOO during 2022 (brutal year, -18%). She bought MORE shares when prices fell. By year-end, she'd accumulated extra positions worth $750+ just by staying disciplined.

6/ The math: When prices drop, your $750 buys more shares. When prices rise, it buys fewer. You automatically smooth your average cost across cycles without trying.

7/ The real win? You don't panic-sell. When VOO recovered in 2023, Maya's entire position was up 15%—because she kept buying through maximum pessimism. That's wealth-building.

8/ DCA works best when it feels worst. Market terror = regular buying at discount prices. Most investors have this backwards—they stop investing when fear peaks.

9/ Pro setup: Automate everything. Set your brokerage to auto-transfer + auto-buy on the same day each month. Remove decisions. Remove emotions. Remove regret.

10/ Start today if you haven't. $100/month into VOO/FZROX/SWPPX on the 15th, automatically. In 30 years, you won't care what happened this month. You'll care that you never stopped.
