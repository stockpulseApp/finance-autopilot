## 1) X THREAD (10 tweets)

```
1/ The Coldcard incident exposed $88M in crypto. But self-custody is STILL safer than exchanges—if done right. Here's how to actually secure your holdings without losing everything. 🧵

2/ First, understand what you're actually protecting: your seed phrase. 12-24 words that regenerate every private key. Lose it = lose your crypto forever. No password reset. No support team. This is why setup matters more than anything else.

3/ TIER 1 (Hot Wallets): $0-2K
Mobile wallets like Blue Wallet for spending money. Think of it like cash in your wallet—convenient but vulnerable. Don't store your life savings here.

4/ TIER 2 (Hardware Wallets): $2K-100K
Trezor, Ledger, BitBox, or Foundation devices. Private keys stay on the device. Even malware can't access them. Buy direct from manufacturer. Never from Amazon resellers.

5/ Critical steps for hardware wallets:
• Generate seed ON the device (never import)
• Write it on paper or metal (NEVER digital storage)
• Test recovery BEFORE moving funds
• Store backups in 2+ geographic locations

6/ TIER 3 (Multisig): $100K+
Single device = single point of failure (see: Coldcard). Use 2-of-3 multisig across different manufacturers in different locations. Complex? Yes. Necessary at serious wealth levels? Also yes.

7/ The $88M lesson: buy from trusted vendors used by institutions (Ledger, Trezor). One manufacturer having a flaw doesn't break your security if you're using multisig. Diversification matters.

8/ Catastrophic mistake #1: Digital backups
Never screenshot your seed phrase. Never save to cloud storage. One compromised Apple ID = gone. Use paper or engraved metal. Period.

9/ Catastrophic mistake #2: Single location backup
House fires exist. Floods exist. Burglaries exist. Keep copies in different geographic locations. Home safe + bank safe deposit box = redundancy.

10/ Before moving significant funds, TEST YOUR RECOVERY. Write down seed → delete wallet → restore from backup. This 5-minute test has saved people six-figure mistakes. Do it every time.
```
