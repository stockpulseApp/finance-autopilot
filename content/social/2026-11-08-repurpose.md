## 1) X THREAD (10 tweets max)

1/ The $6.3B Binance hack in Nov 2026 sparked "not your keys, not your crypto" panic. But here's the truth: self-custody done wrong is MORE dangerous than leaving crypto on an exchange. It's not about WHERE you hold it—it's about HOW.

2/ Self-custody shifts total responsibility to you. No password reset. No fraud department. No insurance. One mistake = permanent, irreversible loss. That's why setup matters infinitely more than anything you do after.

3/ The #1 killer: losing your seed phrase (the 12-24 words that backup your wallet). Storing it in a photo, screenshot, or cloud file? That's like leaving your house keys on a park bench. It's online = it's reachable.

4/ The RIGHT way: Write your seed phrase on paper or engrave it on metal. Create 2 exact copies. Store them in different geographic locations. One safe-deposit box won't cut it if your house burns down.

5/ #2 killer: fake wallet apps. One Reddit user lost $42K by downloading a wallet clone. She skipped verification and moved everything at once. First rule: download from ONLY official websites or verified app store developers.

6/ #3 killer: using a "hot wallet" (internet-connected) for large amounts. Software wallets are convenient but exposed to malware. Hardware wallets cost $50-200 but keep your keys offline. Worth it if you're holding $5K+.

7/ The verification test that saves lives: delete your wallet, reinstall it using your backup seed words. If your funds reappear, your backup works. Never learned this? You're one mistake away from catastrophic loss.

8/ Real talk: if holding your crypto safely requires you to become an ops-security specialist, the tools still have a long way to go. For most people, a reputable exchange + insurance might be the honest choice.

9/ The nuance exchanges won't tell you: self-custody IS safer from exchange hacks. But it's more dangerous from user error. Know which risk profile fits YOU.

10/ Bottom line: self-custody works. But only if you treat it like nuclear launch codes, not like a Coinbase account. Setup matters. Everything else is just maintenance.
