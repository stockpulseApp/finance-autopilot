## 1) X Thread (10 tweets max)

**Tweet 1:**
One typo. That's all it takes to lose $10k in crypto forever. Unlike your bank account, there's no dispute button, no fraud department, no second chance. Self-custody is powerful—but it's also unforgiving. Here's how to do it right. 🧵

**Tweet 2:**
The core problem: crypto was designed to eliminate intermediaries. No bank. No custodian. Just you and your private keys. The trade-off? You become the bank. Your keys = your coins. Lose them, and they're gone forever.

**Tweet 3:**
The FTX collapse showed us the other side of the coin: $8B in customer assets evaporated overnight. Centralized exchanges fail. But careless self-custody fails faster. The answer isn't picking one—it's doing self-custody *right*.

**Tweet 4:**
Mistake #1: Storing your seed phrase digitally. Screenshots. Cloud notes. Password managers. All vulnerable. Rule: If it's online, it can be hacked. Write it down. Stamp it in metal. Never screenshot it.

**Tweet 5:**
Mistake #2: Skipping the test transaction. Moving $15k? Send $5 first. Verify it arrives. Then send the rest. Costs $2 in fees. Prevents five-figure errors. That math is easy.

**Tweet 6:**
Mistake #3: Buying a pre-configured hardware wallet from sketchy sellers. Red flag = arrives with seed phrase already generated. Scammers sell wallets where they already know the words. Buy direct from manufacturer only.

**Tweet 7:**
Mistake #4: Storing large amounts in hot wallets (phone apps, browser extensions). They're convenient—and connected to the internet. Use them for small amounts only. Real money goes in cold storage.

**Tweet 8:**
Mistake #5: Over-approving dApp permissions. That "swap" button? It might be asking for unlimited access to drain your wallet later. Always check what you're authorizing. Use Revoke.cash to audit old approvals.

**Tweet 9:**
Real example: Jordan bought a Ledger on Amazon (not Ledger's site), photographed his seed phrase, then connected to a fake yield DeFi site. His wallet emptied in 12 seconds. All avoidable mistakes.

**Tweet 10:**
Self-custody isn't hard. It's just unforgiving. Setup matters more than everything you do after. Get the fundamentals right—secure seed phrase, test transactions, trusted hardware, limited hot wallet balance—and you own your crypto. For real.
