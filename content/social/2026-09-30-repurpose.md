**Tweet 1:**

**Tweet 2:**
The baseline math: Claim at 62 = 70% of your benefit. Claim at 67 = 100%. Claim at 70 = 124%. Using real numbers: $1,400/mo vs $2,000/mo vs $2,480/mo. On the surface, 70 wins. But wait...

**Tweet 3:**
Here's what changes everything: the break-even age. By claiming at 62, you collect $134k before the 70-year-old sees anything. The delayed claimer doesn't catch up until age 80-82. If you die at 76? Early claiming wins.

**Tweet 4:**
Meet Maria, 62, excellent health, both parents lived past 90. Best move? Wait until 70. The extra $528/month grows to $158k+ over 25 years. Her larger SS checks in her 80s protect against longevity risk.

**Tweet 5:**
Meet James, 63, type 2 diabetes, health concerns. His doctor isn't optimistic about living past 80. Claim now. The guaranteed $1,520/month lets him stop liquidating his 401(k) during market recovery. The math is different when break-even feels unrealistic.

**Tweet 6:**
Married couples get a hidden advantage: claim early/claim late strategy. Lower earner takes reduced benefits early. Higher earner delays to 70. When the higher earner dies, spouse gets 100% of their benefit as survivor benefit. Maximizes security.

**Tweet 7:**
**Four scenarios where early claiming actually makes sense:**
1️⃣ You need cash flow now and lack substantial savings
2️⃣ Health/family history suggests below-average longevity
3️⃣ Still working (benefits reduced anyway)
4️⃣ Afraid of future cuts (rational fear)

**Tweet 8:**
The real question isn't "what age gives the biggest check?" It's "what strategy delivers the most *lifetime income for my specific situation*?" That's personal. Cookie-cutter advice breaks down fast.

**Tweet 9:**
Critical reminder: If you delay Social Security past 65, you still need to enroll in Medicare separately. Missing the window means permanently higher premiums for life. Don't let the Social Security strategy sabotage your healthcare costs.

**Tweet 10:**
The bottom line: Run the numbers for *your* situation. Consider your health, savings, marital status, and longevity odds. The "right" claiming age isn't universal. Sometimes the math says wait. Sometimes it doesn't. Know the difference.
