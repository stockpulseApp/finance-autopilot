## 1) X Thread (10 Tweets)

```
1/ Most people waste their biggest wealth-building advantage: tax-advantaged accounts. The difference between smart sequencing and random contributions? ~$1.5M over 30 years. Here's the hierarchy that actually works 🧵

2/ TIER 1: Employer 401(k) match (only)
Get the free money, then stop. If they match 4%, contribute 4%. That's a guaranteed 100% return. Don't leave it on the table, but don't over-contribute yet.

3/ TIER 2: HSA (if eligible)
The single best tax-advantaged account in the U.S. code. Triple tax advantage: contributions pre-tax, growth tax-free, withdrawals tax-free. Max it aggressively if you have a high-deductible health plan.

4/ TIER 3: Roth IRA
$7,000/year ($8,000 at 50+). Zero income tax on withdrawals ever. A 30-year-old who maxes this from now until 65 at 8% returns? ~$1.4M tax-free. No income limits? Do the backdoor Roth.

5/ TIER 4: Max HSA + Roth IRA
Combined 2025: $4,300 + $7,000 = $11,300/year. That's ~$940/month. Should be the baseline for anyone serious about financial independence.

6/ TIER 5: Additional 401(k) contributions
$23,500/year limit. Only after tiers 1-4. Traditional contributions cut your taxable income today. Makes sense if you're in a high bracket now and expect lower in retirement.

7/ TIER 6: Mega Backdoor Roth (if available)
High earners with after-tax 401(k) conversions can funnel $38,500+ into tax-free Roth space. Only ~20% of plans offer it, but game-changing if you have it.

8/ TIER 7: Taxable brokerage
No contribution limits. Fully liquid. Less tax-efficient, but once you've maxed everything above, this is where excess capital goes. Tax-loss harvest aggressively.

9/ The math: $940/month across HSA + Roth from age 30-65 = $1.4M+ in tax-free wealth alone. Add employer match and 401(k) above? You're looking at $4M+. Sequence matters.

10/ Not personal advice—everyone's situation differs (income, health, employer match, state taxes). If you're making six figures or have complex equity comp, hire a CFP + CPA. If you're starting out? Use this framework.
```
