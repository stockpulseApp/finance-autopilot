# House Hacking Repurposed Content

## 1) X Thread (10 Tweets Max)

1/ Your first home doesn't have to cost you everything. House hacking—buying a multi-unit property, living in one unit, renting out the rest—lets first-time buyers offset their mortgage with tenant income. The math is wild. 🧵

2/ Here's the problem: Median home price = $420k. Mortgage rates = 6%+. Down payment needed = 20%. Most first-time buyers can't math their way in. House hacking changes the equation by turning your tenants into equity-builders.

3/ The genius part? You still qualify for owner-occupied financing (3.5% down FHA, 0% VA loans) even though you're buying an investment property. That's consumer rates on investor returns.

4/ Real example: Duplex at $450k. You put down $15.75k, live in one unit, rent the other for $1,650. Your monthly mortgage = $3,200. Your net cost after rental income = $1,550. That's LESS than the $1,800 apartment you were renting.

5/ Over 5 years: A renter spends $108k and owns nothing. A house hacker spends $108.75k, builds $88k in equity, and gets $12k in tax deductions. That's a $99k swing.

6/ The tradeoffs are real though. You're a landlord now. Shared walls/property lines. Tenant drama at 9pm. Fair housing laws. Maintenance emergencies. You need temperament + systems to make this work.

7/ Not every property works. Use the 1% rule: monthly rent should be at least 1% of purchase price. A $400k property should rent for $4k+ total. If the math doesn't pencil, skip it.

8/ Location > everything. You need strong rental demand + a neighborhood where YOU want to live. Check local landlord laws, lead regs, and eviction rules before you buy.

9/ House hacking isn't for families who need privacy or buyers in weak rental markets. But for first-timers in expensive metros who can handle being a landlord? It's the fastest path to real estate wealth.

10/ The bottom line: House hacking turns the impossible home-buying equation into a wealth-building machine—if you run the numbers first and accept the landlord responsibilities. Not for everyone. Perfect for some.
