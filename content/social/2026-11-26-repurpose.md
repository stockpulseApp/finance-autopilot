## 1) X Thread (10 tweets)

```
🧵 Self-custody sounds simple: hold your own keys, control your coins.

In practice? One mistake = permanent loss. Here's how to do it right.

---

1/ The rule is unforgiving: if you control the keys, you control every way to lose them.

An exchange hack affects thousands. A lost seed phrase? Just you. No customer service. No recovery desk.

---

2/ Every protection an exchange provides, you must replace:
- Key security ✓
- Infrastructure maintenance ✓
- Account recovery ✗ (there is none)
- Regulatory backstop ✗ (you're on your own)

---

3/ Meet Jordan: $90k in crypto, hardware wallet, 24-word seed phrase written on ONE recovery card in ONE desk drawer.

Apartment floods. Card destroyed. No backup.

$90,000: permanently inaccessible.

---

4/ The 5 failure modes that cost people millions:

1️⃣ Single point of failure (one backup, one location)
2️⃣ Digital seed storage (screenshots, notes, cloud)
3️⃣ Signing malicious transactions (drainers, fake approvals)
4️⃣ Fake support scams
5️⃣ Poor OPSEC around large balances

---

5/ The setup matters more than anything you do afterward.

Use reputable wallets (Ledger, Trezor, Blue Wallet, MetaMask).
Generate seed phrases OFFLINE.
Never screenshot or type the phrase.
Never trust anyone asking for it.

---

6/ Defense in depth:

✓ Hardware wallet for cold storage
✓ 2+ physical backups in separate locations
✓ Metal or paper (survives fire/flood)
✓ Optional 25th-word passphrase for large holdings
✓ Test recovery before moving full balance

---

7/ The gap between theory and practice has cost people millions.

Lost phrases. Phished wallets. Device failures. Social engineering.

Self-custody isn't hard. It's just unforgiving.

Get the setup right once. Then you're actually secure.

---

8/ Control over private keys ≠ elimination of risk.

Hardware, software, processes—they all introduce dependencies.

Safeguarding assets requires ongoing attention as tech and threats evolve.

This isn't "set and forget." It's informed, deliberate habits.

---

9/ If you self-custody:
- Use established, audited wallet software
- Verify URLs (no search ads, Telegram links)
- Store backups geographically
- Never connect cold storage to sketchy transactions
- Segregate operational and long-term holdings

---

10/ Self-custody delivers sovereignty.

It also demands responsibility.

The coins you control are the coins only you can lose.

Do it right. The setup is a one-time investment that protects six or seven figures for decades.

Do it wrong? One mistake. That's all it takes.
```
