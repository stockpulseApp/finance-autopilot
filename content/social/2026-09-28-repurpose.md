```
1/ Most people don't realize WHERE you save matters as much as HOW MUCH.

Sarah, a $145K software engineer, was putting $500/month into a taxable brokerage account. Smart move—except she was leaving thousands on the table.

Here's the tax-advantaged hierarchy 👇

2/ THE GOLD TIER: HSA
Triple tax advantage (deductible contributions, tax-free growth, tax-free withdrawals). If you have a high-deductible health plan, max this FIRST.

$8,550/year × 20 years @ 7% = $350K+ (equivalent to $460K taxable)

3/ THE SILVER TIER: 401(k) with employer match
Free money beats tax optimization. A 50% match on 6% of salary = instant 50% return before market gains.

Always capture the full match. It's the closest thing to free money in finance.

4/ THE BRONZE TIER: Roth IRA
$7,000/year, tax-free growth forever, no RMDs in your lifetime. Lower contribution limits than 401(k)s, but unbeaten flexibility for wealth building.

Best for younger earners in moderate brackets (22-24%).

5/ BEYOND THE MATCH: Traditional 401(k) & IRA
Still valuable—every $1K saves you ~$240 in tax (24% bracket). But you'll owe ordinary income tax on all withdrawals.

Tax diversification matters: mix traditional + Roth for retirement flexibility.

6/ SPECIALIZED: 529 Plans
Tax-free growth for education. Many states offer income tax deductions. SECURE 2.0 now allows unused funds to roll into Roth IRAs.

Best if you have kids and want guaranteed college funding.

7/ THE REALITY CHECK: Taxable Brokerage
Last tier, but not worthless. No contribution limits, immediate access, favorable long-term capital gains rates (15-20% vs. 37% ordinary).

Only open this AFTER maxing tax-advantaged space.

8/ THE ALLOCATION FORMULA (first $50K/year):
1. HSA: $4,300 (if eligible)
2. 401(k) match: $7,200
3. Roth IRA: $7,000
4. Traditional 401(k): $17,300
5. Taxable: $14,200

This maximizes tax efficiency across your entire portfolio.

9/ THE NUMBERS: $10K invested at 7% for 30 years
Taxable account (24% tax drag): ~$65,000
Roth IRA (zero tax): ~$76,000

Same contribution. Same returns. $11,000 difference because of account structure.

Where you save MATTERS.

10/ Your move: Audit your current savings allocation. Are you capturing employer match? Maxing HSA? Using Roth before taxable?

Small changes to sequencing now compound into six figures over 30 years.

Get the full breakdown in the link 🧵
```
