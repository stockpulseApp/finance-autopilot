8/ Married couples have a special advantage: Lower earner claims early for household income. Higher earner delays to 70.

When the higher earner dies, survivor gets the larger benefit. Best of both worlds.

9/ The uncomfortable truth: 25%+ of people claim at 62. Most advisors recommend waiting. But financial reality + longevity risk look different for everyone.

Your answer depends on health, savings, and family history—not generic advice.
