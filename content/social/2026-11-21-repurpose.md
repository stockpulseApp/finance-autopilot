The secret? Don't use it for current bills. Invest aggressively, save

3/ 🥉 ROTH IRA
$7,000 annual limit, but no RMDs, penalty-free access to 
contributions, and tax-free inheritance for heirs.

Can't contribute due to income limits? Backdoor Roth converts a 
traditional IRA contribution immediately—no income cap.

4/ 🔓 MEGA BACKDOOR ROTH
If your 401(k) plan allows after-tax contributions, convert up to 
$46,000 additional to Roth annually (varies by employer match).

Only for high earners with significant cash flow, but unlocks 
unlimited Roth access regardless of income.

5/ ⚠️ The Mistake Most People Make:
They default to whatever their employer offers and stop there.

Maxing HSA + 401(k) match + Roth IRA + mega backdoor (if 
available) = $70,000+ annual tax-advantaged investing for some 
households.

Your account hierarchy depends on YOUR tax bracket and goals. 
No one-size-fits-all strategy wins.

Work backwards: max employer match first (guaranteed return), then 
HSA (triple tax-free), then Roth (flexibility), then 401(k) overflow.
