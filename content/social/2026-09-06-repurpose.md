## 1) X THREAD (10 Tweets Max)

**Tweet 1:**
Self-custody sounds empowering: you control the keys, you control the coins. But full control = full responsibility. The blockchain has no undo button, no customer service, no password reset. One mistake and millions vanish forever. Here's how to avoid becoming a cautionary tale. 🧵

**Tweet 2:**
The core truth: a self-custody wallet means YOU—and only you—hold the private keys. Leave crypto on an exchange? You own a database entry, not the actual coins. Exchange collapses = you're an unsecured creditor in bankruptcy court.

**Tweet 3:**
Your wallet generates a cryptographic key pair. The private key authorizes spending. It typically comes as a 24-word seed phrase like "glove whisper ocean." Lose that phrase + lose the device = your crypto is gone forever. No reset link exists.

**Tweet 4:**
❌ BIGGEST MISTAKE #1: Storing the seed phrase digitally. A screenshot. A cloud note. A password manager. All defeated by hacks/ransomware. Hackers literally search cloud storage for files named "seed" or "wallet backup." Paper only. Metal plates for large amounts.

**Tweet 5:**
❌ MISTAKE #2: Sending your full balance on first transfer without a test. A typo. Wrong network. Wrong address. Funds vanish into an address no one controls. Send $10 first. Confirm it arrives. Then send the rest. One network fee to avoid total loss.

**Tweet 6:**
❌ MISTAKE #3: Trusting a pre-configured device. Buying a "new" hardware wallet on eBay with a seed phrase already filled in? Compromised. Buy direct from manufacturer. Generate the seed phrase yourself during first setup. Always.

**Tweet 7:**
❌ MISTAKE #4: Keeping a single backup in one location. House fire. Flood. Burglary. Paper destroyed = crypto gone. Experts recommend minimum 3 backups in separate locations: home safe, parents' house, bank safety deposit box.

**Tweet 8:**
❌ MISTAKE #5: Forgetting the passphrase or weak device PINs. Adding a 25th-word passphrase for extra security? Great—until you forget it. Then your Bitcoin is inaccessible even with the seed phrase. Write it down. Store it separately.

**Tweet 9:**
The alternative—leaving everything on an exchange—introduces its own catastrophic risks: platform insolvency, regulatory seizures, exchange hacks. The question isn't WHETHER to self-custody but HOW to do it without becoming your own worst enemy.

**Tweet 10:**
Self-custody is not difficult, but it is unforgiving. Setup matters far more than anything you do afterwards. Use reputable wallets. Write seed phrases on paper or metal—never screenshots. Keep multiple copies in separate locations. That's it. You're sovereign.
