## 1) X Thread (10 tweets max)

```
🧵 Most freelancers undercharge by 40-60%. Here's why your employee salary is a terrible pricing benchmark—and the real math behind rates that actually work.

1/ Your $50K salary ≠ $24/hour freelance rate. When you were employed, clients paid $150/hr for your work. That $126 gap? Health insurance, manager salary, office lease, paid time off—all your problem now.

2/ Self-employment tax alone jumps from 7.65% to 15.3%. Add health insurance ($5.4-7.8K/year), lost benefits, and 27 paid days off you need to earn in 233 working days instead of 260. You need a 40%+ rate increase just to break even.

3/ The naive calculation: divide your salary by 2,080 hours. Result: poverty by November. 

The real calculation: $72K salary + $28.5K business costs ÷ 1,200 ACTUAL billable hours (not fantasy hours) = $83.75/hour MINIMUM.

4/ Most freelancers only bill 20-30 hours/week. The other 10-20 go to business dev, admin, accounting, learning. If you count those as "billable," your effective rate drops 40-50%.

5/ Project-based pricing beats hourly every time. Quote $4,500 for 40 hours of work at $100/hr—finish in 35 through efficiency and you just gave yourself a raise. Hourly punishes you for getting better.

6/ Value-based pricing is the endgame: that email sequence generating $180K in sales? $1,200 charge = underpriced. $8K (4.4% of value created) = defensible. Clients understand ROI.

7/ Upwork/Fiverr extract ~20% in fees. You need to charge $125/hr to net $100/hr. Use them for portfolio-building only, then pivot to direct clients. Marcus made $14.8K on Upwork, netted $11.4K after fees—then never logged in again.

8/ If no one ever calls you expensive, you're leaving 30-50% on the table. The client who flinched at $5K for a sales funnel probably hemorrhages $5K monthly on wasted ads anyway.

9/ Retainers = income stability. $3-5K/month guaranteed access = 70-80% of your revenue needs on autopilot. You can be selective about the rest.

10/ You're not asking for a favor. You're solving an expensive problem. Price like it.
```
