## 1) X THREAD (10 Tweets Max)

**Tweet 1:**
Your $2,400 mortgage feels impossible on a first-time buyer salary. What if you didn't have to pay it alone? House hacking—buying a property and renting part of it—is how Gen Z is cutting housing costs 50-100%. Here's the real numbers (not Instagram fantasy). 🧵

**Tweet 2:**
Model 1: Buy a duplex/triplex/fourplex. Live in one unit. Rent the others. FHA loans let you put down just 3.5% on properties up to 4 units—same terms as a single-family home, but your tenants pay your mortgage.

**Tweet 3:**
Real example: $280K duplex in Indianapolis. $9,800 down. Monthly mortgage: $2,150. Second unit rents for $1,400. Your net housing cost: $750/month while building $280K in equity. No landlord above you. You ARE the landlord.

**Tweet 4:**
Model 2: Buy a single-family home. Rent out bedrooms. Less income potential than multifamily, but more location flexibility. $350K house in Raleigh, NC. Rent two bedrooms @ $800 each. Net cost: $1,050/month instead of $2,650.

**Tweet 5:**
What Instagram skips: You're now a landlord. Midnight plumbing calls. Tenant drama. Vacancy risk. A $4,000 roof repair? You eat the full cost. This isn't passive—it's a second job with irregular hours.

**Tweet 6:**
You need cash reserves before attempting this. 3-6 months of full mortgage payments sitting idle. One difficult tenant can turn your wealth-building strategy into a daily nightmare. This strategy requires sweat equity, not just financial optimization.

**Tweet 7:**
Exit strategy complexity: When you move, you're selling an investment property. Capital gains taxes apply to the rental portion. The unit you occupied may still qualify for primary residence exclusion, but consult a CPA first.

**Tweet 8:**
House hacking only works in certain markets. You need affordable multifamily properties, strong rental demand, and rent-to-price ratios that support positive cash flow. LA's $1.5M starter homes? House hacking math breaks.

**Tweet 9:**
Best markets for house hacking in 2026: Midwest cities (Indianapolis, Columbus, Cleveland), Southeast (Raleigh, Nashville), and parts of Texas where duplex prices stay under $350K and rents stay strong.

**Tweet 10:**
Bottom line: House hacking cuts housing costs dramatically—if you can handle being a landlord in your own building, tolerate reduced privacy, and maintain cash reserves. Not for everyone. But for disciplined first-time buyers? It's a game-changer. 📊
